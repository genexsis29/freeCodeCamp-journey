Noodles

Pakistani Knorr Noodles are a beloved, quick-fix comfort food known for their signature vibrant and aromatic spice blends like Chatpata and Chicken. Ready in just under three minutes, they offer a perfect balance of savory warmth and tangy heat that appeals to all ages. Whether enjoyed as a comforting soupy broth or a dry, flavorful masala snack, they are an absolute staple in Pakistani households.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Knorr Noodles (Chicken or Chatpata)
  • 1.5 to 2 cups Water
  • 1 packet Knorr Masala / Taste Maker
  • Optional add-ons: ½ tbsp chili sauce, ½ tbsp soy sauce, or a pinch of chopped green onion

Instructions

  1. Boil the water first
  2. Add the taste maker immediately
  3. Cook for exactly 2 to 3 minutes
  4. Choose your consistency

1. Boil water: Pour 1.5 to 2 cups of water into a small pot and bring it to a rolling boil over medium heat.

2. Add noodles: Break the Knorr noodles in half (if you prefer shorter strands) and drop them into the boiling water.

3. Add flavor: Stir in the Knorr masala/taste maker packet right away. Add the optional soy sauce or chili sauce now if you want an extra spicy kick.

4. Cook: Let it cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are soft and tender.

5. Adjust consistency: If you like soupy noodles, leave a little bit of broth. If you prefer dry/masala noodles, let the water dry up completely.

6. Serve: Remove from heat and serve hot in a bowl.
